Section 7 — Before you book
Short practical blocks.
Altitude matters
San Pedro itself is already high, while excursions such as El Tatio go much higher.
Have a dedicated:
San Pedro de Atacama Altitude Guide
This is important enough to deserve its own article and internal links from every high-altitude tour.
Tours start early
El Tatio in particular means a very early departure, because the geysers are normally visited around sunrise. Viator
Entrance fees aren't always included
This is a surprisingly valuable affiliate angle.
Some tours exclude park/reserve entrance fees even when transport, breakfast or lunch is included. Current Piedras Rojas and El Tatio products provide examples of this. Viator
I would have call-outs on every review page:
Check before booking
- entrance fee included?
- hotel pickup?
- English-speaking guide?
- breakfast/lunch?
- return point?
- altitude restrictions?
