## Cejar, Ojos del Salar and Tebinquinche in one outing

This route suits travellers who want a lagoon afternoon with several stops and a cocktail. Floating is advertised for the Cejar visit, but reserve rules and operating conditions still determine water access. Do not infer that every water stop on the route allows swimming.

Admission is additional and the listing asks for cash payment in advance, with booking at least 24 hours ahead. Confirm the full reserve-ticket total, the payment arrangement and your pickup address. The described finish is Caracoles 600, which may differ from a door-to-door hotel return.
