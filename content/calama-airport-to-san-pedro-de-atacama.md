## The straightforward arrival plan

San Pedro de Atacama has no airport for scheduled flights; the airstrip near town takes private aircraft only. You fly to Calama and go on by road, and our [guide to getting to San Pedro](/getting-there/) covers flights, buses and overland routes.

Book a transfer from **Calama Airport (CJC) to San Pedro de Atacama**, then provide your flight number, arrival date and accommodation details. The featured {{< gyg-link tour="190838" >}}Calama Airport transfer (4.6/5 from 850 reviews){{< /gyg-link >}} offers shared and private options in air-conditioned vehicles. Select the one that suits your group; the lowest displayed price is not for an exclusive vehicle.

The road journey is advertised at about one and a half hours on the paved highway. That is not your total airport-to-room time: baggage collection, meeting the driver, waiting for other shared passengers and hotel stops can add time. Leave a generous margin before an afternoon excursion.

With the transfer sorted, compare the trips that fill the first days: **Valle de la Luna** at sunset, the **El Tatio geysers**, the **Piedras Rojas** and altiplanic lagoons, and **Laguna Cejar**.

## Ways from the airport compared

| Option | Door-to-door from landing | What to check | Suits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shared transfer | Often two to three hours, including bags and waiting for other passengers | Flight waiting policy, other stops and address coverage | Solo travellers, couples and most first-timers |
| Private transfer | Shorter, because it leaves when you are ready | Actual group price, capacity and flight-delay terms | Families, groups of four or more, late arrivals and heavy luggage |
| Taxi from the airport | About the length of the drive, if a car is waiting | Agree the fare before you set off; cars are not always available | A backup if a booked transfer falls through |
| Taxi plus bus | The longest, with two legs and a fixed timetable | The bus timetable from Calama town and your luggage | Budget travellers landing in daytime |
| Rental car | The drive plus the rental pickup | Insurance, route permissions, parking and unfamiliar-road driving | Independent travellers planning self-drive days |

A private transfer is still subject to flight and road delays. A rental car also brings responsibilities that a transfer avoids, particularly if you are tired after a long journey. You can do the main sightseeing circuits [without a car](/san-pedro-de-atacama-without-car/) by using booked excursions.

## Arrival day, step by step

1. **Book your flight to Calama (CJC)**, not to "San Pedro". A daytime landing gives you the most transfer choices.
2. **Book the transfer at least two days ahead** and enter your flight number. Some local operators stop taking airport bookings within 48 hours.
3. **Withdraw cash at El Loa Airport.** San Pedro has only a handful of ATMs, and they can run out on busy weekends.
4. **Collect your bags and find the driver** at the arrivals exit, as described below.
5. **Settle in for the drive**, about one and a half hours. A right-hand window seat gives the first views of the volcanoes.
6. **Check where you will be dropped off.** Shared transfers cover a set zone around town. If you stay in an outlying *ayllu* (rural settlement), you may be dropped at a meeting point.
7. **Take it easy on day one.** San Pedro is at about 2,400 m. Drink water and keep the high-altitude tours for later; the [altitude guide](/altitude/) explains why.

## Meeting the driver

The selected listing describes a driver waiting by the arrivals exit with a name card. Keep the booking confirmation and supplier contact available offline. If you cannot find the representative, use the number in that confirmation; leaving with an unrelated driver means the original reservation will not cover you.

The driver language is listed as Spanish, and the supplier notes that some drivers speak little or no English. Have your hotel name and address written clearly. Confirm any late-night arrival, flight change or delay handling directly with the supplier.

## If you land late at night

Late flights have fewer shared departures, and a shared vehicle may wait for the last passengers before it leaves. You have three options:

1. **Book a private transfer** and give the supplier your flight number, so a delay does not leave you without a ride.
2. **Stay a night near Calama airport** and take a morning transfer. This also suits travellers who arrive tired after a long-haul connection.
3. **Do not count on a taxi.** Few wait for the last flights, and San Pedro has no ride-hailing apps.

Whichever you choose, take cash at the airport ATM before you leave, because the ATMs in San Pedro can be empty by the time you arrive.

## Returning to Calama Airport

Select **San Pedro to Calama Airport** for the departure leg. The listing says hotel pickup is usually at least three hours before the flight. Follow the pickup time in your confirmation and your airline’s check-in requirement, not the length of the drive.

Send the correct departure flight number and check the date carefully for flights around midnight. A one-way booking does not include the return unless you have explicitly selected and paid for it.

- **Early-morning flights.** For a flight at around 6 a.m., pickup falls in the middle of the night, and some shared services do not cover the earliest departures. Book a private transfer, or spend the last night in Calama.
- **Holidays.** Some operators run reduced hours on 24, 25 and 31 December and 1 January. Check the cover for your flight time before you book.
- **After El Tatio or Uyuni.** Do not put a geyser tour or a Bolivia return on the same day as an afternoon flight. Leave a buffer day.

## Cheaper ways to San Pedro

No bus leaves from the airport itself. The budget route is a taxi into Calama town, then a bus to San Pedro from one of the company terminals (Frontera del Norte, Atacama 2000 and Tur-Bus run the route). It saves money, but the two legs, the luggage and the fixed timetable make it a daytime option only. Check the carrier’s current timetable, because published schedules disagree.

A group of four or more can also split a private transfer, which often brings the cost per person close to a shared seat with none of the waiting.

## Luggage, children and access

The included allowance is one suitcase and one carry-on per person. Oversize luggage is not allowed in the listing. If you carry a bike, large equipment bag or more luggage, ask before booking; spare space is not guaranteed.

The current product is not marked as wheelchair accessible. Discuss vehicle access, seating and assistance before reserving if those affect your party. Ask about child seats and the correct passenger categories instead of assuming a shared vehicle provides them automatically.

## Plan your first and last touring days

A flight arriving in the morning does not guarantee you can make a [Moon Valley departure](/moon-valley-tours/) that afternoon. Give yourself time to arrive, eat and orient yourself. For the final day, avoid stacking a [Piedras Rojas full-day tour](/piedras-rojas-tours/) ahead of a flight that depends on the estimated return being exact.

A central hotel can simplify both transfers and tour meeting points. If you choose an outlying property, confirm coverage with the transfer provider and separately with each excursion supplier. The [where-to-stay guide](/where-to-stay/) explains that trade-off.
