## The straightforward arrival plan

Book a transfer from **Calama Airport (CJC) to San Pedro de Atacama**, then provide your flight number, arrival date and accommodation details. The featured listing offers shared and private options in air-conditioned vehicles. Select the one that suits your group rather than assuming the lowest displayed price is for an exclusive vehicle.

The road journey is advertised at about one and a half hours. That is not your total airport-to-room time: baggage collection, meeting the driver, waiting for other shared passengers and hotel stops can add time. Leave a generous margin before an afternoon excursion.

## Shared or private?

| Choice | Why choose it? | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Shared transfer | Simple transport for solo travellers or couples | Flight waiting policy, other stops and address coverage |
| Private option | More control over vehicle sharing | Actual group price, capacity and flight-delay terms |
| Rental car | Independent travel after arrival | Insurance, route permissions, parking and unfamiliar-road driving |

A private transfer is still subject to flight and road delays. A rental car also brings responsibilities that a transfer avoids, particularly if you are tired after a long journey. You can do the main sightseeing circuits [without a car](/san-pedro-de-atacama-without-car/) by using booked excursions.

## Meeting the driver

The selected listing describes a driver waiting by the arrivals exit with a name card. Keep the booking confirmation and supplier contact available offline. If you cannot find the representative, use the number in that confirmation rather than leaving with an unrelated driver and expecting the original reservation to cover it.

The driver language is listed as Spanish, and the supplier notes that some drivers speak little or no English. Have your hotel name and address written clearly. Confirm any late-night arrival, flight change or delay handling directly with the supplier.

## Returning to Calama Airport

Select **San Pedro to Calama Airport** for the departure leg. The listing says hotel pickup is usually at least three hours before the flight. Treat the confirmed provider time and your airline’s check-in requirement as the instructions, not the minimum drive duration.

Send the correct departure flight number and check the date carefully for flights around midnight. A one-way booking does not include the return unless you have explicitly selected and paid for it.

## Luggage, children and access

The included allowance is one suitcase and one carry-on per person. Oversize luggage is not allowed in the listing. If you carry a bike, large equipment bag or more luggage, ask before booking rather than assuming spare space will be available.

The current product is not marked as wheelchair accessible. Discuss vehicle access, seating and assistance before reserving if those affect your party. Ask about child seats and the correct passenger categories instead of assuming a shared vehicle provides them automatically.

## Plan your first and last touring days

A flight arriving in the morning does not guarantee you can make a [Moon Valley departure](/moon-valley-tours/) that afternoon. Give yourself time to arrive, eat and orient yourself. For the final day, avoid stacking a [Piedras Rojas full-day tour](/piedras-rojas-tours/) ahead of a flight that depends on the estimated return being exact.

A central hotel can simplify both transfers and tour meeting points. If you choose an outlying property, confirm coverage with the transfer provider and separately with each excursion supplier. The [where-to-stay guide](/where-to-stay/) explains that trade-off.
