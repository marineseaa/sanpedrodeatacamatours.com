## A road transfer that bridges the airport and town

This listing offers both shared and private transfer options between Calama airport and San Pedro. Its lowest displayed price should be read as a starting point across options, not as a quote for your own private vehicle. Select the direction, party size and vehicle option before comparing totals.

The advertised journey takes about one and a half hours. Air conditioning and taxes are included, with one suitcase and one carry-on per traveller listed. Oversized luggage and wheelchair access are excluded; arrange a different confirmed service if those are essential.

## Meeting point and return

Airport arrivals exit/name board, or hotel pickup for departures. Driver language listed as Spanish.

Use the operator’s final confirmation for the exact time and address. If your accommodation is outside the centre, send its full address before assuming the advertised collection area covers it.

## Fit this into your trip

For airport arrivals the description uses a name board near the exit. For departures it generally schedules collection at least three hours before the flight, but the confirmed pickup message controls. Provide the correct flight number and accommodation address and ask how delays affect a shared departure.

## Eligibility and details to confirm

Not wheelchair accessible in the current listing. Oversize luggage is not allowed.

Before booking, check the entry-ticket arrangement, pickup and return point, language, meals and any age or altitude restrictions against your party’s needs. Read the cancellation deadline shown on the option you actually select. A weather-related route change is a separate question from voluntarily cancelling a reservation.
