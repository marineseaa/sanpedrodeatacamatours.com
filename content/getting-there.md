## Fly to Calama, then continue by road

El Loa Airport uses the code **CJC** and serves Calama. San Pedro de Atacama is a separate destination reached by road. The featured transfer lists a journey of around one and a half hours, with shared or private vehicle options.

Check your airline’s actual schedule and baggage terms directly. This guide does not promise a current route frequency or airfare. For most visitors planning a short desert stay, the important point is that landing at Calama is not the same as arriving at the tour meeting point in San Pedro.

## Reserve the correct transfer direction

For arrival, choose airport to accommodation and provide the flight number and exact hotel address. For departure, choose accommodation to airport and provide the departing flight details. A one-way reservation does not include both journeys.

Read our [detailed Calama transfer guide](/calama-airport-to-san-pedro-de-atacama/) for the meeting procedure, baggage allowance and shared-versus-private comparison. The listing minimum is a per-person starting price; it should not be read as the total for a private vehicle.

## Shared transfer, private transfer or rental car?

A shared transfer keeps the arrival simple but may involve waiting for passengers and several hotel stops. A private option can reduce the number of shared stops, subject to the actual service conditions. Neither can guarantee a flight will arrive on time or a road will be delay-free.

A rental car may suit an independent route, but think about fatigue after flying and whether you actually need the vehicle for the excursions you plan. You can cover the main sights [without a car](/san-pedro-de-atacama-without-car/) using organised day trips.

## Other overland arrivals

If arriving by intercity bus or continuing from another country, verify the actual route, terminal and current timetable with the carrier. Border and highland-road conditions can affect international travel. We have not published a guessed bus frequency, fare or border schedule.

A [Uyuni expedition](/uyuni-tours-from-san-pedro-de-atacama/) is not interchangeable with a simple cross-border transport ticket. Check where it starts and ends, how long it takes and whether its highland overnights suit your wider journey.

## Keep the first afternoon flexible

An early flight can still be delayed, and the shared transfer has its own logistics. If you reserve [Moon Valley](/moon-valley-tours/) for arrival day, leave enough time to check in, eat and reach the departure. Ask about the cancellation terms before buying a tour that depends on a tight connection.

Do not make [El Tatio](/el-tatio-geysers-tours/) your automatic first-morning booking after a late arrival from low altitude. Read the [altitude guide](/altitude/) and make the first part of your stay less demanding.

## Protect the departure day too

The transfer supplier usually collects passengers at least three hours before their flight, according to the selected listing. Your actual confirmation and airline guidance govern the timing. Avoid planning a full-day excursion immediately before a flight based only on an estimated return.

When deciding [how many days to stay](/how-many-days/), count the full touring days between these arrival and departure stages. That gives a more honest schedule than counting every calendar date as available for sightseeing.
