## Sunrise at the geysers, with a very early start

This trip suits travellers willing to trade sleep for a dawn visit to the El Tatio geothermal field. Steam, cold air and the morning light are the attraction. Breakfast is included, but reserve admission is not, and the operator asks for that payment in advance.

Pickup is described as approximately 04:30–05:10, with urban San Pedro accommodation served and outlying ayllus excluded. Confirm your exact address and the current pickup message the evening before. The source has a confusing AM/PM return-time entry, so use its six-hour duration only as an estimate.

## Meeting point and return

Pickup normally 04:30–05:10 within urban San Pedro. Nearby ayllus and Toconao are excluded. Confirm central return point and time.

Use the operator’s final confirmation for the exact time and address. If your accommodation is outside the centre, send its full address before assuming the advertised collection area covers it.

## Fit this into your trip

The route goes above 4,300 metres. Put it after lower-altitude time, check the operator’s age and health restrictions, and keep the rest of the day gentle. Stay on marked visitor paths around geothermal features and pack serious warm layers for the pre-dawn departure.
