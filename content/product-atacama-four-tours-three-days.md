## Four named excursions coordinated over three days

The four excursions are Moon Valley, Piedras Rojas and the highland lagoons, El Tatio, and Baltinache. This can simplify arranging several operators, but it is a sightseeing package rather than a holiday with accommodation, airport transfers and every ticket included.

The listing includes the Chaxa, highland-lagoon and Piedras Rojas admission arrangement for bookings after 7 October 2025. Moon Valley, El Tatio and Baltinache admission remain separate. Check the final written total and ask how the Baltinache water-access suspension affects your itinerary.

## Meeting point and return

Urban San Pedro pickup only. The operator contacts you via WhatsApp to allocate departure days.

Use the operator’s final confirmation for the exact time and address. If your accommodation is outside the centre, send its full address before assuming the advertised collection area covers it.

## Fit this into your trip

Request the sequence before booking flights or a separate astronomy tour. This package does not include stargazing. Its generic child-age wording is less restrictive than the separate El Tatio rule, so obtain eligibility confirmation for the full route rather than relying on the package’s headline age field.

## Eligibility and details to confirm

The generic panel says under-7s, but the El Tatio note prohibits under-9s. Pregnancy, heart/respiratory problems, high blood pressure and wheelchair use are excluded.

Before booking, check the entry-ticket arrangement, pickup and return point, language, meals and any age or altitude restrictions against your party’s needs. Read the cancellation deadline shown on the option you actually select. A weather-related route change is a separate question from voluntarily cancelling a reservation.
