## First count your usable days

This plan assumes two full days in San Pedro, with airport travel outside them. If your “two days” includes landing at Calama at lunchtime and leaving the next afternoon, reduce the plan. The [airport transfer](/calama-airport-to-san-pedro-de-atacama/) takes time beyond the flight itself, and shared transfers may involve waiting and additional hotel stops.

The bookable cards above are **separate excursions**, not one package. Match their departure times and cancellation terms before paying for all three.

## Day 1: settle in, then Moon Valley

**Morning:** take a slow start, organise water and any separately required reserve ticket, and check your operator messages. If arriving that morning, leave time to reach the hotel and eat rather than booking a departure that depends on every connection being on time.

**Afternoon:** take the [Moon Valley excursion](/moon-valley-tours/). The shared tour leaves from an agency; the ticket-and-cocktail alternative includes hotel pickup. Decide which makes your day easier and budget for admission if needed.

**Evening:** eat in the central Caracoles/plaza area after returning. We recommend an early dinner rather than a restaurant reservation with a rigid start time. If the sky tour’s confirmed schedule leaves a comfortable break, stargazing can follow; otherwise use the second evening.

## Day 2: a lagoon choice, then the night sky

**Morning:** keep time free for rest and town. You can use this interval to confirm bathing access and the day’s return time rather than discovering a mismatch at pickup.

**Afternoon:** choose [Cejar](/laguna-cejar-tours/) for a floating-focused circuit if bathing is confirmed open. Choose [Baltinache](/baltinache-tours/) for its turquoise scenery, accepting that floating is currently suspended. Booking both makes less sense than choosing the one that answers your main interest.

**Evening:** allow time to change, have a simple dinner in the centre and reach the [stargazing meeting point](/stargazing-tours/). If the lagoon tour and astronomy session do not leave that margin, move stargazing to day one or drop the afternoon outing. A two-day visit has little room to recover from cloud cancellations.

## Should you substitute a highland day?

If you are already acclimatised and specifically want the highlands, substitute [Piedras Rojas](/piedras-rojas-tours/) for the entire second day. You will lose the lagoon half day and may prefer an early night afterwards. Do not add the full-day circuit on top of the schedule.

[El Tatio](/el-tatio-geysers-tours/) is another possible priority swap, but its early departure and altitude make it a poor default immediately after arriving from the coast. Two days is not a reason to ignore suitability or operator restrictions. See the [altitude guide](/altitude/) before deciding.

## Meals and costs to organise

Keep breakfast at your accommodation and lunch in town unless a selected tour explicitly supplies them. Ask whether your hotel can provide an early breakfast if you choose a predawn excursion. A cocktail or snack inclusion is not the same as a meal.

Budget for your accommodation, airport transfers, separately charged park tickets, meals and the tour reservations. We do not show a synthetic “two-day package price”: the products have different dates, options and conditions, and the total depends on what you choose.

## What an extra day buys you

The [three-day itinerary](/3-day-itinerary/) adds flexibility for one major highland excursion. Four full days make it easier to separate the highest trips and leave a spare evening for astronomy. If both El Tatio and Piedras Rojas are essential, consider changing the length of the stay instead of compressing them into your first 48 hours.
