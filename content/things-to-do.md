## Start with the experiences that feel different

A useful shortlist is not the longest list. [Moon Valley](/moon-valley-tours/) gives you dunes and sculpted salt formations, [Piedras Rojas](/piedras-rojas-tours/) adds a full highland circuit, and [stargazing](/stargazing-tours/) changes both the setting and the pace. These are our first comparisons for a visitor who wants variety; the [full tour comparison](/tours/) adds altitude, start times and entry tickets.

If [El Tatio](/el-tatio-geysers-tours/) is your must-see, plan around its predawn departure and altitude rather than appending it to a late night. It is a different experience from Piedras Rojas, but visiting both requires enough usable days and suitable conditions.

## Desert landscapes: Moon Valley and the highlands

Moon Valley is the approachable first-tour choice because the standard format is about four hours and leaves part of the day free. Compare agency departure with a more inclusive ticket-and-pickup product. The higher price may cover costs the cheaper one leaves to you.

The Piedras Rojas circuit is a much bigger day, typically linking Miscanti and Miñiques, red-rock shores, Socaire and Chaxa. It suits landscape-focused travellers who are ready for the altitude. Check each stop by name; “lagoons” in a title does not identify every lake or reserve included.

## Sunrise steam at El Tatio

The geyser field is the reason to tolerate the cold early start. The featured listing adds breakfast and Andean wildlife scenery, with entry charged separately. Save it until later in your stay where possible and read the current age and health restrictions before reserving.

If the high altitude or departure time makes it unsuitable, that does not make your trip incomplete. Use the day for a lower-desert activity or rest instead of buying an excursion simply because it appears on every attraction list.

## Stargazing and night photography

The astronomy options combine a guided explanation, telescope use and a souvenir photo. The Andean Astronomy product offers a different interpretive focus from a general sky introduction. Neither is an ALMA research-observatory visit.

The Moon, cloud and the actual observing time affect what you see. Give yourself another available evening when practical, and do not assume a rescheduled slot is guaranteed. Our [best-time guide](/best-time-to-visit/) helps separate month-level advice from the conditions of a particular night.

## Pick one lagoon outing

[Cejar](/laguna-cejar-tours/) is the floating-focused listing to investigate, with Ojos del Salar and Tebinquinche on its route. [Baltinache](/baltinache-tours/) is currently a sightseeing choice because the featured supplier reports bathing suspended from 29 September 2026.

A second lagoon tour may be worthwhile if you particularly enjoy the scenery, but it is not essential on a short trip. The [Baltinache versus Cejar comparison](/baltinache-vs-laguna-cejar/) gives the practical differences.

## A slower half day at Puritama

[Puritama Hot Springs](/puritama-hot-springs-tours/) offers a different kind of water visit: warm thermal pools rather than saltwater floating. Coordinate the entry reservation and transport, since the selected transfer excludes admission. Ask about paths and pool access if mobility affects your choice.

## Leave time for San Pedro itself

Keep a few unbooked hours around the centre for a meal, a quiet walk and the practical tasks that make the excursions easier. This guide does not invent opening times for local museums or restaurants. Check current information locally before making a special trip to a venue.

Choose [central accommodation](/where-to-stay/) if you want to walk to agencies and dinner. Use the [two-day](/2-day-itinerary/), [three-day](/3-day-itinerary/) or [four-day](/4-day-itinerary/) plan to match the shortlist to your actual stay.

## Treat Uyuni as a separate expedition

The [Uyuni return tour](/uyuni-tours-from-san-pedro-de-atacama/) needs four days of its own, crosses into Bolivia and includes remote highland overnights. It is not one more attraction to fit between a sunset and an airport transfer. Compare it only when the journey itself is part of your wider South America plan.
