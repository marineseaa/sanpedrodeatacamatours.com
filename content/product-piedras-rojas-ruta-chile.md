## What to expect on the Piedras Rojas tour

The tour covers the high country south of San Pedro in one loop. Miscanti and Miñiques are two lagoons at about 4,200 metres, below the volcanoes of the same names, and the listing has breakfast served beside them. Piedras Rojas is the red rock on the shore of the Salar de Talar. Laguna Chaxa sits in the Salar de Atacama, inside the national flamingo reserve, and the listing says three species of flamingo feed there. Socaire, a village with farming terraces and a church, and Toconao complete the itinerary.

Visiting these places independently means a long drive on high roads, paying the entry fees yourself and bringing your own food. This tour combines transport from your accommodation, both meals and a guide in one day.

Guides are rated 4.8 out of 5 on GetYourGuide, transportation 4.8 and value for money 4.6. Most reviews praise the guides and drivers, and Sheryl, Denny, Ronald, Nicolás and Celinda are the guides travellers name most often.

Nobody on our team has done this trip yet, so what follows comes from the GetYourGuide listing and from its reviews at every star rating, read in English and in Spanish, Portuguese, German, French and Italian, with the most attention on pickups, closures and meals. Four details affect how the day goes:

- Pickup times are only confirmed the evening before, usually as a window, and a few travellers were collected well after it or not at all.
- Snow, strong wind or storms can close Piedras Rojas or the lagoons, and several reviewers say the change was explained only once the tour had started.
- The return ends in the centre of San Pedro, not at your accommodation.
- Meals are served outdoors at altitude, and breakfast in particular can be cold, windy and dusty.

Ruta Chile sells the tour, and a local San Pedro operator runs the day, so the company you book with and the team in the van are usually different. Fewer than one review in ten is below four stars, and more than four in five give five stars.

For a single full day on the high plateau, this tour covers the main sights with both meals included. It does not include the park fees, a drop-off at your hotel or any promise that every high stop will be open.

## Five tips before you book

1. **Send your full accommodation address and a WhatsApp number as soon as you book.** The local operator uses both to send the pickup window and to find you in the morning.
2. **Bring about CLP 29,000 per person in cash for the entry fees.** The listing gives this as an approximate figure. At current exchange rates it is about US$30, which takes the day to roughly US$116 per person before tips.
3. **Schedule the tour after your first two days in San Pedro.** The lagoons are about 1,800 metres higher than town. The listing rules out under-8s, over-70s, pregnant travellers and people with heart problems or high blood pressure, and the [altitude guide](/altitude/) suggests lower trips such as the Valle de la Luna first.
4. **Pack a warm layer, sun protection, water and a snack.** Mornings at the lagoons can be below freezing, and the van can be hot on the way back. One reviewer had breakfast at 7:30 and lunch at 2:30, with nowhere to buy food in between.
5. **Keep a spare day in case the high sites close.** If the guide announces a shorter route, ask before you set off whether you can move to another date or get a partial refund.

For the lower days before it, the usual choices from San Pedro de Atacama are sunset in the [Valle de la Luna](/moon-valley-tours/), floating at [Laguna Cejar](/laguna-cejar-tours/) and an evening of [stargazing](/stargazing-tours/). The sunrise trip to the [El Tatio geysers](/el-tatio-geysers-tours/) goes higher still, so it also belongs later in the stay.

## What the day is actually like

The listing gives a pickup at around 06:00 to 06:30 and a return to San Pedro at about 16:00. The first stop is about an hour's drive away. The stop times on the itinerary add up to roughly four hours, so most of the rest of the day is spent in the minibus and at the two meals.

Groups are limited to 14 people. A recent reviewer who was collected last found the back seats cramped for a full day, so if you are being collected near the end of the round, ask whether a front seat is free.

Guiding is offered in Spanish, English and Portuguese, and on mixed days the guide alternates between languages. A few travellers said their guide spoke mostly Spanish, so check the language shown on your booking.

The order of the stops can change. The itinerary starts at the high lagoons, but some groups go the other way round, beginning at Chaxa and reaching the lagoons later in the day.

## Stop 1: Laguna Miscanti and Laguna Miñiques

The itinerary gives 30 minutes at each lagoon, and this is where breakfast is usually laid out. Reviewers describe sandwiches with avocado, cheese and ham, scrambled eggs, cake, and tea, coffee or cocoa. Several reviewers name the two lagoons as their favourite stop.

It is also the coldest stop. Reviewers mention cold wind at breakfast, one described dust blowing over the food, and another recorded −4 °C on a March morning. Dress for it before you leave the van.

## Stop 2: Piedras Rojas

The itinerary allows an hour at Piedras Rojas, the red rock beside the Salar de Talar, although one reviewer described 30 to 40 minutes on the ground.

Piedras Rojas is one of the stops that occasionally closes. Snow and strong wind can shut access, mainly from June to August, and summer storms can do the same from December to March; our [best time to visit guide](/best-time-to-visit/) covers the seasons. On those days the guide runs a shorter route, sometimes with a substitute stop. Reviewers who lost stops said they were told only during the tour, and several said they received no partial refund. One traveller whose day was cut short by snow in September 2025 was offered a different lagoons-and-salt-flats trip and refunded the difference.

## Stop 3: Laguna Chaxa

Chaxa gets 30 minutes on the itinerary, and the highlights place lunch near the flamingo reserve. The numbers of flamingos vary, and travellers also mention vicuñas, foxes and other animals spotted from the van on the way.

## Stop 4: Socaire

Socaire is a 30-minute stop on the itinerary, with farming terraces and a village church. Opinions are more mixed here than at the lagoons. One reviewer said the church was seen only from outside, and a few found the shorter roadside stops less interesting than the main sights.

## Stop 5: Toconao

The itinerary lists an hour at Toconao, on the road back to San Pedro, although the listing's written description does not mention it. Treat it as a break and photo stop on the return.

## Pickup and the hand-off to the local operator

How smoothly the morning goes depends mostly on the hand-off between the company you book and the one that runs the tour. Ruta Chile is the listed activity provider. Recent reviewers mostly name Atacama Magic as the team in the van, and older reviews name Layana Travel and Andes Travel.

The pickup time usually arrives by WhatsApp or email the evening before as a window, such as 06:00 to 06:40. Travellers collected last waited 30 to 40 minutes, and a few in 2025 and 2026 were never collected, one after waiting outside from 6 until 8. Several reviewers said messages to the office went unanswered, and the first word came from the guide shortly before pickup. In one 2024 case, a booking was cancelled as a no-show after the traveller missed a request for hotel details sent the day before.

If no pickup window has arrived by the evening before, message the operator through the GetYourGuide app. If nobody has arrived by the end of the window, contact GetYourGuide support straight away so the problem is on record.

## Other practical things to know

- The listing puts "drop off in the center of San Pedro" under its exclusions, and travellers describe being dropped at the agency in the centre. If your accommodation is outside town, arrange a taxi for the last stretch.
- Tips are not included. One recent reviewer said this was the only tour in San Pedro where the guide asked for one.
- Recent reviewers describe vegetarian and coeliac meals being provided on request. Put the requirement in the booking.
- A few lower-rated reviews felt the price was high for a day of five stops, most of them short.

## Is the Piedras Rojas tour worth it?

Yes, if you have a free day after acclimatising and want the high lagoons, Piedras Rojas and Chaxa in one trip. The $86 price includes transport for a 10-hour loop, a guide, breakfast at the lagoons and a cooked lunch. With the entry fees, the day comes to roughly US$116 per person before tips.

The value drops on a day when snow or wind closes Piedras Rojas or the lagoons, because the route is shortened while the price stays the same. It is a weaker choice if you need a fixed pickup time and a drop-off at your door, or if anyone in your party is under 8. The Triple Tour costs slightly more and leaves out lunch, and a [multi-day package](/multi-day-tours/) books several highland and desert trips in one go.

If you book, check the guiding language on your confirmation and look at the forecast for the high plateau the day before.
