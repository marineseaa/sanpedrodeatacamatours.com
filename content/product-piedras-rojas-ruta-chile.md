## A complete landscape day with two meals

Choose this version when you want the classic red-rock and highland-lagoon circuit with both breakfast and lunch arranged. Miscanti and Miñiques, Piedras Rojas and Chaxa are different environments; seeing them together involves a substantial amount of driving between outdoor stops.

The alternative Triple Tour lists breakfast and snacks, so compare the food service as well as the starting price. This Ruta Chile listing describes an early morning pickup and an afternoon return; keep the whole day clear and ask for clarification of its inconsistent return-point wording.

## Meeting point and return

San Pedro accommodation pickup; arrange the return point. The listing places centre drop-off in its exclusions, so confirm this detail.

Use the operator’s final confirmation for the exact time and address. If your accommodation is outside the centre, send its full address before assuming the advertised collection area covers it.

## Fit this into your trip

Use a lower excursion such as Moon Valley first, then schedule this highland day later in your stay. The stops around 4,200 metres can feel demanding even when the walking is short. Bring water, sun protection and warm layers for windy meal stops.

## Eligibility and details to confirm

The restrictions panel excludes under-8s and over-70s, pregnancy and several health or mobility conditions. Another note says under-7s; use the stricter limit until confirmed.

Before booking, check the entry-ticket arrangement, pickup and return point, language, meals and any age or altitude restrictions against your party’s needs. Read the cancellation deadline shown on the option you actually select. A weather-related route change is a separate question from voluntarily cancelling a reservation.
