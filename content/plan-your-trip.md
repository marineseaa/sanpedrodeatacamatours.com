## First decide how much time you really have

New to the region? Our [Atacama Desert travel guide](/atacama-desert-travel-guide/) covers what the desert is, the wider region and typical costs. Use [how many days in San Pedro](/how-many-days/) to separate full touring days from airport dates. Then choose the [two-day](/2-day-itinerary/), [three-day](/3-day-itinerary/) or [four-day itinerary](/4-day-itinerary/) that matches your available time.

The distinction matters for packages too: a three-day bundle needs suitable departure days, and does not automatically start after your flight arrives. Ask for the operator’s expected schedule before locking in other reservations.

## Arrange the arrival and your base

Read [getting there](/getting-there/) for the flight-to-road sequence and the [Calama airport transfer guide](/calama-airport-to-san-pedro-de-atacama/) for direction, meeting point and luggage details. A day tour starting in San Pedro does not include airport transport unless that service is stated.

Choose [where to stay](/where-to-stay/) around the pickup areas. Central accommodation is practical for agency starts and late returns. Outlying properties can be quieter but need confirmed transport arrangements. The [no-car guide](/san-pedro-de-atacama-without-car/) explains how to combine those pieces.

## Check altitude before filling the calendar

The [altitude guide](/altitude/) distinguishes the town, highland day trips and overnight journeys into Bolivia. Put the highest excursions later where possible, follow age and health restrictions and leave room to change the plan if you feel unwell.

Avoid putting a late astronomy return directly before the predawn El Tatio pickup. More reservations do not automatically make a better trip; a real dinner and a full night’s rest can be the more useful addition.

## Choose dates and pack for the experience

The [best-time guide](/best-time-to-visit/) covers highland summer rain, winter cold and moon-sensitive astronomy. Its date checker offers seasonal advice and an approximate lunar calculation, not a live weather or road report.

Use [what to pack](/what-to-pack/) for the difference between a cold dawn, a sunny landscape stop and standing at telescopes. Check bathing rules before packing for a lagoon outing, especially while Baltinache’s floating suspension remains in effect.

## Make the final tour choices

The [complete tour comparison](/tours/) and [things-to-do guide](/things-to-do/) help narrow the list. If you are deciding between the salt lagoons, use [Baltinache versus Cejar](/baltinache-vs-laguna-cejar/) rather than treating older swimming photographs as current access information.

A [multi-day package](/multi-day-tours/) may simplify scheduling, but inspect every inclusion and extra fee. For [Uyuni](/uyuni-tours-from-san-pedro-de-atacama/), reserve a separate block of days and check border documents, highland sleeping and the correct endpoint.

## Keep the confirmations accessible

Save the supplier contacts, meeting instructions and any reserve tickets offline. Tell each operator your exact address and relevant requirements. Confirm the final details directly with the company running the activity; this website provides comparisons and planning advice, not reservation management.
