## Transport to the pools, with a separate entry ticket

This listing provides the journey to Puritama and back, with guide assistance described. Pool admission is explicitly excluded. It is therefore a transport arrangement rather than an all-inclusive hot-springs ticket, and the available vehicle space does not by itself confirm pool availability.

Arrange a compatible pool admission session before committing to timings. Towels, food and drinks are not included either. Confirm your pickup and return address, bring your bathing kit and allow time to change before the transport leaves.
