## A small-group alternative for the classic circuit

This is an alternative for travellers who want Piedras Rojas, the highland lagoons and Chaxa in one day. The listing caps the group at 14. That limits group size, but does not turn a shared itinerary into a private trip or guarantee extra time at a particular viewpoint.

Breakfast and snacks are listed; lunch is not an automatic inclusion. Compare that with the Ruta Chile option before deciding which headline price represents better value for you. Reserve admission is also separate and the listing requests advance payment through the agency.

## Fit this into your trip

Leave a full day for the route and avoid booking a flight immediately after the expected return. If a particular lagoon is essential to your trip, ask how the operator handles closures or road restrictions before paying.

## Eligibility and details to confirm

Listed as unsuitable for under-10s, pregnant travellers and people with high blood pressure.

Before booking, check the entry-ticket arrangement, pickup and return point, language, meals and any age or altitude restrictions against your party’s needs. Read the cancellation deadline shown on the option you actually select. A weather-related route change is a separate question from voluntarily cancelling a reservation.
