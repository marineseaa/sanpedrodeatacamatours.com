This static website does not provide a visitor account, checkout or contact form. Bookings take place on external websites.

## Third-party services

Pages load GetYourGuide’s analytics and availability services. Those requests can disclose technical information such as your IP address, browser information and the referring page to GetYourGuide. Affiliate links contain this website’s partner and campaign identifiers. GetYourGuide may use cookies or similar technologies according to its own settings and policies. Read [GetYourGuide’s privacy policy](https://www.getyourguide.com/privacy_policy) before using its services.

The design requests fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google’s font services to retrieve them, unless a cached copy is available. See [Google’s privacy information](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Hosting and browser controls

The hosting provider may record technical access logs when serving the site. Your browser may cache images, styles and scripts. Use your browser’s privacy settings to manage third-party cookies and stored website data. Blocking scripts may prevent availability widgets from loading; the ordinary booking links remain available.

## What the date checker does

The seasonal date checker calculates its result in your browser. This site’s own code does not submit your selected date to a server or save it in a visitor account. It is an approximate planning aid, not a weather forecast.

This notice describes the services included in this website build as of 9 October 2026. The providers’ own policies govern information processed after following an external link.
