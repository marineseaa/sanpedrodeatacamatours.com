## A simpler Moon Valley sightseeing option

Choose this shared trip if you can get to the agency meeting point yourself and prefer to compare a lower tour-only price with the reserve ticket added separately. It includes guided sightseeing and transport, rather than a hotel-pickup-and-cocktail package.

The listed meeting point is Tocopilla 418. Reserve admission needs to be bought separately in advance; ask which ticket and time slot match the tour. Food and drinks are not listed as included, so carry water and arrange a meal around the outing.

## Meeting point and return

Meet at Tocopilla 418, San Pedro town centre; central drop-off. Verify departure time for your date.

Use the operator’s final confirmation for the exact time and address. If your accommodation is outside the centre, send its full address before assuming the advertised collection area covers it.

Sunset timing changes through the year. The source’s seasonal departure text is inconsistent, so use the confirmation message for the actual meeting time. Allow time to walk to the agency and keep your onward plans flexible until you know the return point.
