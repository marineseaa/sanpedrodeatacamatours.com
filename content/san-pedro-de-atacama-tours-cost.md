## The main differences

- **A good all-round choice for the big day out:** the <a href="https://www.getyourguide.com/san-pedro-de-atacama-l1862/san-pedro-de-atacama-red-stones-and-altiplanic-lagoons-t402852/" data-gyg-href="https://www.getyourguide.com/san-pedro-de-atacama-l1862/san-pedro-de-atacama-red-stones-and-altiplanic-lagoons-t402852/" target="_blank" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener noreferrer">Red Rocks and Altiplano Lagoons day trip</a>, from US$96 with breakfast, lunch and all the entry fees included (4.8/5 from 150+ reviews).
- **Add the entry fee before you compare.** Two well-reviewed Piedras Rojas tours start at US$86 and US$89, but you pay about CLP 29,000 (about US$29) of fees on top, so they end up costing more than the US$96 tour.
- **The fee is the same whoever you book with.** CONAF and the Atacameño communities set it: CLP 10,800 at Valle de la Luna, CLP 15,000 at El Tatio and CLP 15,000 at Laguna Cejar.
- **Stargazing costs the least of the main tours.** Sessions with telescopes, drinks and blankets start at US$29; the dearer ones keep the group much smaller.
- **Everything here is priced per person.** Two people pay two fares and two entry fees; five tours over three days cost about US$590 for a couple at the starting prices.
- **The three-day package costs more than its four tours booked one by one** with the same operator, once the fees it leaves out are added.

## The full day we would book first {#availability}

The highland day to Piedras Rojas, the Miscanti and Miñiques lagoons and Laguna Chaxa is the longest and dearest of the usual day tours, and it is where the entry fee changes the order most. Pukarumi's day trip includes the Chaxa entry and the Piedras Rojas and highland lagoons entry in its US$96, along with breakfast, lunch and hotel pickup.

## What you get as you spend more

Mostly hotel pickup, food and a smaller group. The lagoons, the geysers and the view from the Valle de la Luna dunes are the same on every tour, and so is the entry fee.

**Under about US$50: short trips near town and stargazing.** You often meet at the agency or a pickup point, and the fee is usually extra. The {{< gyg-link tour="466455" >}}Valle de la Luna shared tour{{< /gyg-link >}} is the typical example, from US$35 plus its ticket.

**About US$50 to US$80: hotel pickup, a meal or a drink, and often the fee.** The {{< gyg-link tour="345946" >}}El Tatio sunrise tour{{< /gyg-link >}} at US$72 includes breakfast and the entry, which is what most tours at this price add.

**About US$85 to US$100: the full day on the high plateau.** These are 10-hour days with breakfast and lunch, and the difference between them is mostly whether the CLP 29,000 of fees is in the price.

**Above US$100: small groups at night and multi-day trips.** The <a href="https://www.getyourguide.com/san-pedro-de-atacama-l1862/atacama-stargazing-tour-into-our-universe-t1163787/" data-gyg-href="https://www.getyourguide.com/san-pedro-de-atacama-l1862/atacama-stargazing-tour-into-our-universe-t1163787/" target="_blank" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener noreferrer">Stargazing Tour Into Our Universe</a> costs from US$181 for a group of four to six with photos sent the next day, against US$29 for a group session.

## Should you pay more?

Pay more for hotel pickup if you are staying outside the centre and would otherwise walk to a meeting point in the dark, and check that your address is covered. Pay more for a small stargazing group if photographs of the night sky are the reason you came. On the long highland day, compare the totals with the fees added and pick on reviews; within a few dollars, the tours visit the same lagoons. For Valle de la Luna and stargazing, the cheapest well-reviewed tours give you the same sunset and the same sky.
