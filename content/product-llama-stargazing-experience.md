## A telescope session with a souvenir photograph

This experience combines sky interpretation, telescope viewing and group or individual night photographs, with drinks, snacks and blankets listed. Choose it for an approachable introduction to the sky rather than assuming it is a dedicated photography workshop with equipment tuition.

The booking option changes the meeting arrangement: pickup is offered in some options and a school E-26 meeting point is also described. Return is near central San Pedro, with outlying ayllus excluded. Check the exact option and your walk or transfer home before reserving.

## Meeting point and return

Meeting/pickup depends on option. School E26 is a listed meeting point; return coverage excludes outlying ayllus.

Use the operator’s final confirmation for the exact time and address. If your accommodation is outside the centre, send its full address before assuming the advertised collection area covers it.

## Fit this into your trip

A bright Moon changes what can be seen. The operator may run a Moon-and-planets session rather than the dark-sky experience you pictured. Ask about the observing window, cloud cancellation and photo delivery, and keep a spare evening if astronomy is your trip’s main purpose.
